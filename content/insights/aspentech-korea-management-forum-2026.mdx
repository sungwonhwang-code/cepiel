On 4 September 2026 I joined the **Korea Management Forum 2026**, hosted by
Emerson | AspenTech at The Plaza Seoul. The theme was *Leading Through Volatility to
Achieve Industrial Excellence* — an evening for leaders from
across the process industries.

It was a bit of a homecoming. Early in my career I spent two years as a
staff consultant with AspenTech UK's Advanced Process Design group, so it was good to
be back in the room — this time from the other side of the table.

The panel was titled **"Beyond the AI discourse: purpose-built industrial AI for market
responsiveness, operational efficiency, and autonomous operations."** Jinkyu Yoon
moderated, and I sat alongside Dr. Heiko Claussen and Dr. Vikas Dhole — incidentally a
fellow alumnus of the Process Integration group at Manchester.

The title captures the point I care most about. Generic AI is easy to talk about and
hard to deploy in a plant. What works in chemical and energy operations is AI that is
*built for the process*: grounded in first-principles models, fed by real plant data,
and judged by whether it moves an operating KPI — energy, yield, reliability, or
emissions.

That is the gap we work on at [CEPI Lab](https://cepi.inha.ac.kr) and at CEPiEL, where
CEPiDTS™ combines process simulation, machine learning, and optimization into
digital twins for operating plants. It was valuable to compare notes with a company
that sees the same problem from the software side.
